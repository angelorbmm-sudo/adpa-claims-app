# Evidence to scope and contents drafts — October 13, 2026

## Delivered

Each current human-confirmed Photo finding offers Create draft scope item and/or Create draft contents item according to the finding's classification. Both / Uncertain findings allow explicit selection. The collapsed Draft scope & contents items panel in the common Evidence Inbox opens automatically after creation.

Drafts carry the confirmed description, room, matching physical component/item reference, legible identity, uncertainty, original requested details, photo-region locator and immutable original provenance. Current completed Missing Evidence request responses for that finding are carried only when visible to the creating role and still authorized. Their completion does not automatically replace unknown model or measurement facts.

The creator can edit a working draft, add authorized supporting originals, and record a manually established quantity only with a unit, explicit count/measurement basis, a linked source and its locator. Unknown quantities and identities begin blank. Edits retain the prior field snapshot and source IDs in server history. Matching contents references prevent duplicate active drafts within a creating role across different photos; distinct items require distinct references.

Submitting requires a confirmed room and source-review note. Draft becomes Ready for review. Its creator or separately authorized ADPA can return it for correction or mark Reviewed for handoff after examining its originals and recording a note. Review may retain explicitly listed gaps; the status is not final estimate, inventory, price, coverage or claim approval. The creator or policyholder can dismiss an obsolete draft with a reason rather than deleting history. Copy source-linked draft brief supports handoff for scoping, inventory and pricing research.

## Permission and integrity rules

Unsubmitted drafts are visible only to their creating role and accepted policyholder. Submitted/reviewed drafts may be read by other participants only when every supporting original is current and authorized to that account. Only its creator edits/submits; other companies cannot forge its review. ADPA review requires current representation and explicit consent for every original. Source/response changes pause drafts, block further edits/reviews and remove them from unauthorized recipients. No draft action shares a file or grants participation.

File ID, digest, upload revision and created time bind every source. Storage availability and metadata are checked on mutations; optimistic job/company/ADPA versions protect saves. Completed-response imports preserve proof and source permission. Unknown or wrong-type room/item associations, unsupported quantities, foreign sources, duplicate references and stale writes are rejected.

Up to 200 drafts/job, 24 supporting originals/draft and 50 history entries/draft within the existing 800 KB state limit. Contents references are not inventory counts. Scope drafts are not Xactimate line items. There are no prices or financial changes in this workflow.

## Downstream boundary

Drafts reside in the shared job's evidenceDrafts state. This task does not automatically write them into final contents inventory, company specialist scopes, pricing candidate tables, carrier packages or Xactimate. The source-linked draft brief and reviewed record are the handoff foundation. Actual publication to those modules remains a separate task with explicit target mapping and duplicate/quantity checks. AI photo examination still awaits Jose's runtime service-key activation; confirmed manual findings can create drafts now.

The common workflow serves every company industry and the policyholder. Existing file consent, private Site audience and final claim release controls stay in force. No email/text or provider request is sent automatically.
