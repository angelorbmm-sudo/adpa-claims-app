# Initial contractor inspection — October 12, 2026

Shared recovery jobs now include Inspection, with a shortcut from My Jobs for approved shared jobs. Approved company managers record a room/roof/exterior/other area, observed condition, actual inspection time/person, explicitly measured positive dimensions (feet/meters), measurement basis, missing information and a question for ADPA. Unmeasured areas leave dimensions blank. No area/volume, price, estimate or Xactimate calculation is inferred. Peter's estimating input remains pending.

Each record requires 1–12 current authorized shared-job originals with source locators. The server retains original digest/revision/created references, actor/timestamp and existing job/company version guards; original/contributor checks guard the mutation. Records are append-only; corrections refer to the company's own earlier record with a reason. Future observations, hidden/changed sources, invalid numbers and unauthorized correction attempts are rejected. Records are bounded to 200 per shared job and existing serialized limits apply.

The inspection follows its supporting evidence permissions. Originating company and accepted homeowner retain factual history, with inaccessible sources omitted and flagged for reconciliation. Other approved companies and separately connected ADPA receive a record only while every original remains visible and current. ADPA also needs a current explicit matching engagement. No raw fingerprints, actor IDs, private original copies or hidden-source metadata are returned. Sharing/withdrawal controls remain in Shared evidence.

Questions and missing-information flags are saved factual fields, not automatically delivered messages/tasks. The form explains this and prohibits private strategy, banking or sensitive policy details. Inspection drafts are unsaved until explicit Save, with leave/switch warnings. This is an initial factual record, not coverage, safety clearance, work authority or a priced contractor scope. No notifications or provider activation. Browser visual QA unavailable; live walkthrough deferred. Domain/API, TypeScript and production build checks validate this change.
