# ADPA check, bucket and depreciation reconciliation

## Finished workflow
- Standalone /payment-review?id=<claim> and embedded panel in Evidence & Pricing / Contents Care. Existing claim Payment Ledger links directly to the new workflow. Morning attention shows unreviewed/unreceived checks, lender/trust-held checks without release evidence and depreciation follow-ups.
- Private upload/select one physical check’s full front, back and payment stub (up to 3 sources). Manual readable-fact entry works without external keys. AI adapter inspects the entire supplied view for check number, issuer, every payee, printed date/claim reference, numeric amount, amount words, memo and uncertainties. Cropping, amount disagreement, joint-payee restrictions and absent memos are uncertainty leads.
- No MICR bank account/routing fields or signature reproduction are extracted. Original images remain private ADPA evidence. Destination/account is a label with optional last four; full numeric account strings are rejected.
- Extracted/manual candidates are unposted. Memo-only keyword hints suggest buckets/categories; an absent memo leaves no suggestion. No hint silently assigns money. Printed claim references are compared locally against owner claim metadata; leads do not move a check between claims.
- Review/correct original facts, numeric-vs-written amount consistency, correct claim, duplicates, bucket and destination. Require issuer, check number, all payees, positive face amount, explicit identity basis and source review. Different printed claim reference requires a checked corroborating claim original; all exceptions remain audited and source-bound.
- Choose existing coverage buckets and service categories, with explicit ACV/RCV/recoverable-depreciation/etc. purpose. Split combined checks into positive allocations adding exactly to the face amount. Pack-out/cleaning/storage do not silently become replacement-property dollars. Funds destination is recorded independently; no transfer or bank authorization occurs.
- Repeated normalized issuer/check number is blocked within the claim and across other ADPA-owned claims. A final SQL duplicate guard protects concurrent cross-claim approvals. Potential existing legacy references require linking/reconciling the existing payment rather than posting another.
- Link/migrate an existing detailed ledger payment only after explicit same-check/status verification. Existing issued/received allocations must be complete, equal the face amount and have source-backed dates/evidence; ambiguous partial receipts cannot be inflated to the full check. Migration retains the old payment ID and prior history.
- Confirmation creates or updates ONE managed row in the existing state.payments ledger. Old coverage/bucket headline totals remain unchanged for reconciliation. New managed rows cannot be changed, forged or removed by ordinary claim writes; legacy ledger edit/void buttons redirect managed corrections to Payment review. Other unmanaged legacy records remain editable.
- Issued, Received, Deposited and Released are separate source-based statuses of that same record. Dates must be valid/chronological and not future; preceding status evidence must be checked. A photo/endorsement never automatically records possession, deposit, clearance or release. Deposited/Released require a separate bank/servicer confirmation original, not the original check image. Status records are manual evidence-based attestations, not live bank verification.
- Changed face amount requires fresh stage review; old stage amounts cannot be silently multiplied by the new face value. Changed claim/bucket identity, missing metadata or altered source bytes block affected reviews/statuses. Held checks are excluded from current known check subtotals and visibly flagged.
- Voided/stopped/reversed reports retain prior facts/stages and require a supporting original/reason. They update ledger inclusion only; the app does not stop, reverse, deposit, endorse or reissue bank instruments. Linked ALE reimbursements must be reconciled before voiding their payment.

## Statements and potentially recoverable depreciation
- Record source-reviewed carrier RCV/ACV, recoverable/nonrecoverable withholding, deductible, prior payments and explicitly stated net payable. Separate Single payment advice from Cumulative issued summary. Never infer payable from a demand or add/deduct prior payments again.
- Link unique reviewed checks in the same bucket. Compare explicit net payable with their issued bucket allocations. Single advice can match a later-issued check; cumulative summaries use the recorded as-of date. Unknown matching issuance stays unknown unless no matching issued checks were explicitly confirmed. Statements are separate historical comparisons, not figures to add together or proof of amounts owed.
- Each bucket has one revisable depreciation workstream rather than duplicate withholding records. Require original policy PDF/locator, carrier withholding/payment source, recovery conditions, documentation/carrier status and next action/internal follow-up date.
- Only checked allocations explicitly marked Recoverable depreciation count as linked issued depreciation payments. Other ACV/service dollars do not reduce withholding. No selected release is unknown unless absence was explicitly checked. Newly recorded unlinked issued depreciation checks hold the remaining figure until reconciled. Over-release does not create a negative or guaranteed entitlement.
- Unreleased figure is potential recorded withholding less linked issued depreciation allocations, subject to source review and policy/repair/receipt conditions. It is not automatically owed or received. Carrier approval/denial/documentation status stays separate from actual check stages.
- Package payment projections now include managed check identity, destination and deposited/released stages. Package warnings include unreconciled check/statement/depreciation review. Sources and actual bank evidence still require human release review.

## Activation and limits
AI uses the existing server OPENAI_API_KEY and OPENAI_MODEL (default gpt-4.1). Production currently has no key: José must activate it. The UI reports this honestly and leaves manual review enabled. AI reads up to 3 PDFs/JPEG/PNG/WebP originals totaling 12 MB; 10 extraction attempts per hour per claim. Different physical checks detected in a batch are rejected for separate review. Strict output schema, untrusted-data instructions, no tools and no automatic posting/sending. Real check-reading accuracy has not been validated with live checks.

No bank/inbox integration is active. No money was transferred, deposited, endorsed, stopped, released or collected; no actual carrier message was sent. Destination labels are not linked bank accounts. Provider-backed verification, bank processing and automated carrier correspondence remain José’s separate integration work.

Existing private evidence and authenticated ADPA-owner access apply. Origin checks, strict schemas, claim-version and exact evidence metadata guards protect saves; originals carry SHA-256 proofs rechecked during source-based actions. Candidate/source snapshots never grant a company/policyholder access to private bank records. No migrations or new dependencies.

Bounds: 200 check records per claim, 20 allocations per check, existing buckets indexed 0–19, 100 statement reviews, 100 recent check revisions, 50 recent statement/depreciation revisions, 300 recent audits and the existing 1 MB claim JSON cap. Claim-number suggestions search up to 2001 owner claim metadata records and report truncation. This is bounded operational bookkeeping, not an unlimited/certified legal archive.
