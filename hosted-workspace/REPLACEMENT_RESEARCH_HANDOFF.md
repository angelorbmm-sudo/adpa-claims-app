# Accepted contents to replacement research — October 13, 2026

## Delivered workflow

Accepted contents items from the reviewed-draft bridge appear in Evidence & Pricing under Accepted contents → replacement research. The destination intake also links directly to pricing after acceptance. Create linked replacement research carries the actual item tag, room, whole count/unit, count basis, source-confirmed specifications and all retained originals into a single Contents pricing candidate. Unknown identity stays blank. Existing target candidates are reused; repeated creation does not duplicate quantities or invalidate a linked review without changed facts.

The existing pricing workflow records seller URLs or uploaded quote originals, observed date, exact/LKQ comparison, availability, unit price, total tax/shipping and exclusions. Live public lookup sends only the user-confirmed generic product query and returns cited research leads; sources must be opened and verified before prices are entered. Evidence files themselves are not sent during web lookup.

ADPA records current human source review, then selects one verified alternative and confirms comparison/count/unit/fees before approving that replacement proposal. The selected provider and extended proposal total are displayed. Approval retains actor, date, note, selected price ID, fact/source snapshot and prior approval history. Source, listing, item, quantity, identity, date or provenance changes stale the approval. Inventory count/unit and physical pricing key must match the accepted item. Every retained source remains attached.

Approval is an internal ADPA replacement proposal: it does not set contents RCV/ACV, calculate depreciation, change coverage demands/payments, promise carrier acceptance or release a claim package. Package pricing records include the recorded selection; current alternative approval is also required by the existing release/readiness warnings for linked replacement candidates.

## Integrity

Only the configured ADPA owner prepares and approves this accepted-item workflow. Claim ownership, same-origin requests, claim version and evidence metadata guard writes. Actual selected item/quote originals are checked for presence and size before approval; retained original digests are verified. Ordinary claim writes cannot forge or delete accepted transfer/provenance or pricing approval records. Duplicate physical keys are checked case-insensitively. Foreign files, missing/currently stale human reviews, mismatched counts/units, unknown identity, unavailable/stale prices and incomplete tax/shipping/exclusion records block approval.

GET freshness uses saved facts/metadata, not repeated hashing of every original. Actual bytes are verified on approval. Standalone quote uploads without retained digest metadata are checked for presence/size; durable immutable storage/enterprise scanning remains production integration work.
