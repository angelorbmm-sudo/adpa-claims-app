# Contents company dashboard

Route: `/contents-company`. The existing `/company` route remains focused on water mitigation. Companies have a server-assigned service type: legacy company records default to Water mitigation; new Contents care companies use the contents workflow. Manager and technician grants, named invitation acceptance, assigned jobs and live revocation reuse the company access layer. There is no database migration for this extension: service type and contents record grants are versioned JSON in existing companies and claims.

ADPA setup creates a company with a named manager invitation, assigns an existing ADPA claim and shares selected originals and linked contents records. The system validates that selected records and their original files belong to this claim. Saving invitations sends no email and does not grant private Site access. No real company, contact invitation or claim was created during implementation.

The focused workflow opens with assigned jobs, due tasks, documentation gaps and ADPA requests. A job shows inventoried record count, items awaiting contractor cleaning review and items reported ready for return. Inventory, pack-out, cleaning, storage and pack-in use the same physical item IDs. Supporting photos, video, audio, documents and receipts use the existing company-scoped upload route and remain in ADPA-owned storage. The inventory exports a draft CSV with spreadsheet formula escaping.

Company-created contents records merge into the existing claim's Contents Care module, with server timestamps and an audit trail. Users edit only records they entered; ADPA can reconcile assigned records. Custody events are immutable in the company workflow. Whole inventory counts, ordered pack-out/return counts, timezone-qualified custody timestamps, and documented home-readiness reviewer and return acceptance are checked. Draft cleaning and storage records can be incomplete, but documentation gaps prevent a clean manager-to-ADPA handoff. Qualified assessment conclusions are entered as documentary reports; the app does not certify safety or prescribe treatment.

Company managers separately confirm the originals they reviewed for their handoff and record service authorization, work-plan and assessment evidence in the assigned company job. These records support the company handoff; ADPA reviews them before recording any final module assessment or document verification. A company check does not mark ADPA's final contents evidence review complete. Company handoff freshness tracks selected contents records, checks, project evidence and original metadata. It does not depend on unrelated water scope. Actual contents record changes still stale ADPA package fingerprints.

Invoices and report versions are manager tasks. Technicians do not receive invoice rows or financial subtotals. Company users cannot record approved/issued invoice amounts; ADPA reconciles those figures. Service records do not authorize settlement or process money. Inventory items and services remain available for ADPA's existing source-backed Evidence & Pricing draft handoff. This build does not activate AI transcription, automatic replacement sourcing, Xactimate or banking.

Verification: 122 automated tests, including 12 additional contents-company tests for custody counts, readiness, sources, role isolation, two-company isolation, revocation, freshness and successful manager/ADPA handoff; TypeScript and production build. Browser/device visual QA is unavailable in the managed environment and still needed before rollout.
