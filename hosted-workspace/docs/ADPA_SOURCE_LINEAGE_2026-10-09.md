## Evidence and pricing delivery

- Every current uploaded evidence file enters a derived pricing queue, including recordings and opaque formats. Files require human triage or supported conversion; no unsupported file is silently marked analyzed.
- Owner-scoped, version-checked pricing API creates linked cost candidates, distinct alternative quotes/listings, source review and audit events. Protected pricing cannot be forged through ordinary claim saves or initial service imports.
- Selected PDFs, images and text can produce draft candidates through the server AI connection when configured. Output IDs/sources/schema are validated; unknown facts stay blank; monetary values are not inferred by extraction.
- Public price research uses a user-confirmed generic query, never private claim context, with clickable source citations. It needs both `OPENAI_API_KEY` and an explicitly selected, web-search-capable `OPENAI_PRICING_MODEL`.
- Price review requires supporting originals, unit/count basis, exact/LKQ comparison, availability, whole-cent prices/tax/shipping, exclusions and a recent source date. The 30-day freshness threshold is an internal review trigger, not a policy rule or claim deadline.
- Reviews are stamped server-side and bound to source identity, candidate facts, source triage and linked record. Edits invalidate the review. Similar descriptions are not deleted automatically; shared physical/work keys flag possible duplicates.
- Candidates appear in preparation findings and can be selected in internal package snapshots. No property value, payment, depreciation, coverage allocation or final demand is automatically changed. Alternatives are never summed together into one demand.
