# Water mitigation company dashboard

Route: `/company`. This is the shared framework for mitigation companies; a contents-company adaptation is a future module.

ADPA setup: create a company with a named manager invitation, then choose an existing ADPA claim and explicitly select originals, rooms and mitigation lines. Shared lines require their rooms and source files. Managers must accept their named invitation and can invite technicians. Accepted technicians require an explicit job crew assignment. Invitation acceptance pins the signed-in account ID; subsequent membership and assignment revocation are checked on every company API request. A company invitation is not Site access: this deployment remains owner private until ADPA separately authorizes a Site audience change.

Job work: staff upload originals (30 MB maximum), record measurements, append immutable observations/drying logs and draft source-backed mitigation lines. Writes merge into the same Water Scope Studio records without persisting a synthetic participant grant. Staff can edit their own measurements and lines. Managers coordinate tasks, receive field submissions and send documented scopes to ADPA. ADPA handoff review is a workflow record, not line acceptance, coverage determination, professional approval or settlement release. Actual line acceptance remains in Water Scope Studio. Repeated physical-work groups across the claim block a clean handoff. Material changes make handoffs stale.

Tasks: an employee response does not close an ADPA request. The request coordinator resolves or cancels it with a reason. Open ADPA requests with company responses and company-to-ADPA scope handoffs appear in the ADPA morning review queue. Due counts use Pacific dates. Company job messages are visible to assigned crew and ADPA and do not send email.

Evidence: company uploads remain in ADPA-owned storage and in the selected company job. They are not automatically shared with another company, the policyholder portal or individual Water Studio participants. Private policy, negotiating drafts, claim financial ledgers and reconstruction scope are excluded from company views. Removing a file from selected sharing revokes company access. Existing independent Water Studio invitations remain independent and must be revoked separately when ending all access.

Billing: source-backed submitted, approved, issued and received amounts remain separate; blanks mean unknown. Company managers record submitted and received; ADPA records approval and issuance. Technicians do not see billing values in the dashboard. Stale or withdrawn sources blank reported values. Billing is operational tracking and does not write the claim ledger, include ADPA fees or process payments. Source changes and actual water-scope changes stale internal claim-package facts; pure company task/access/message changes do not.

Validation: 110 automated tests, TypeScript and production build. Includes identity binding, manager restrictions, technician assignment, revocation, source isolation, hidden IDs, two-company isolation, handoff freshness, money-stage separation and upload ownership. Browser/device visual QA was unavailable in the managed environment; live device review is still needed before staff rollout. Xactimate, AI evidence analysis, transcription and replacement sourcing remain dependent on their separately configured integrations; this dashboard does not activate them.
