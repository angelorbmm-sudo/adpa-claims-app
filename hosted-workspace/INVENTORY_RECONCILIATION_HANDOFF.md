# ADPA carrier-versus-inventory reconciliation

## Completed workflow
The owner-only panel sits beside inventory exports, contact review and submission tracking in Evidence & Pricing and Contents Care. Active comparisons feed the existing morning review queue; archive/reopen with a reason preserves history and removes superseded work from that queue.

1. Choose a reviewed ADPA export, optionally linked to its exact manually reported submission. Verify claim identity and freeze its historical inventory baseline in private R2. Current inventory/pricing edits cannot silently change the submitted demand used in the comparison.
2. New inventory exports now retain a private structured model with a SHA-256 digest alongside PDF/XLSX. Earlier exports are reconstructed from their actual retained ADPA Excel workbook. Submitted baseline start verifies both PDF/XLSX attachment digests. Duplicate baseline references or a different claim number fail.
3. Upload a private carrier XLSX/CSV/TSV/PDF/text original, or select an existing claim original. CSV/TSV MIME types are now supported by the original evidence upload route. No new sharing access is granted.
4. Preview a worksheet, select header/first/last item rows and map columns explicitly. Description is required; unknown fields are unmapped/blank. RCV/depreciation/ACV are extended item USD totals; explicit unit price is separate. Currency, claim and amount basis require review.
5. Import unreviewed carrier rows with original metadata, digest and sheet/row locators. Column mapping is not acceptance. Formula cells, percentage-formatted monetary cells, percentage/unit/payment/deductible/limit headers mapped as extended amounts, duplicate source rows and invalid amounts fail. Summary/payment rows are flagged for exclusion. No ACV, depreciation, quantity or missing carrier amount is invented.
6. Alternatively transcribe a PDF/original line manually, with locator and source quote, or use the conditional strict AI PDF adapter. Editing a transcription retains its prior version and invalidates old comparisons.
7. Review suggested identity matches. Suggestions use descriptions/tags/room and preserve ambiguity; they never accept matches. Select groups supporting one-to-one, one-to-many or many-to-one physical correspondence. A physical ADPA/carrier row cannot be used in multiple groups.
8. Review matching, pack/set/unit/count and tax/shipping/depreciation bases. Choose RCV or ACV and Compared / Agreed / Disputed / Unanswered / Omitted / Excluded with an explanation and source checks.
9. Agreed means aligned known valuation on the selected basis, verified physical counts, no source/value holds and a separately checked carrier agreement original/locator/quote. An estimate match is not, on its own, carrier agreement. This is recorded human agreement review, not legal authority or an approval to settle/pay.
10. Omitted requires a carrier inventory original/import and an explicit full-original search. Absence stays unknown on the carrier side; no carrier zero is inferred. Adding/editing carrier rows invalidates prior omission searches.
11. View extended RCV, depreciation, ACV and quantity differences, lower known same-unit unit prices, unmatched rows and holds. Unknown/held/unapproved legacy requested values never become approved supported demand. RCV/ACV agreed, disputed net/positive differences, unanswered and omitted requested subtotals are separate and partial. No amounts owed, paid or received are computed or modified.
12. Download comparison CSV with descriptions, source IDs/locators, component values, bases, review notes and holds. It can be downloaded while incomplete but is explicitly a comparison with visible holds. Strings are escaped against spreadsheet formula execution; unknown cells stay blank.
13. Once every ADPA/carrier row is reviewed/grouped or excluded with a reason and source/value holds are clear, fill/edit/save a supported supplement draft for review. It lists issues, historical baseline, reviewed figures, carrier quotes and ADPA references. All-agreed/excluded comparisons do not create empty issue supplements.
14. Download the reviewed supplement as text. Source metadata/bytes, frozen baseline, comparison facts and claim identity are rechecked; changes require new review. No external email is sent. Evidence originals must be attached separately if transmitting to a carrier; app references remain private.

## Source integrity and role limits
- Only the ADPA owner can operate this panel or download its drafts/baselines. Foreign claims and sources are rejected. Generated storage keys are not returned to clients.
- Group review captures SHA-256 proofs of carrier/ADPA originals and any agreement. Metadata and item/group changes produce visible holds; unchanged-metadata byte changes block supplement preparation/download until a fresh source review.
- Request origin, strict schemas, optimistic claim version and evidence metadata guard every save. Failed concurrency cannot write a reviewed comparison. Private unreferenced baseline artifacts can remain after a failed database save; they are not published or evidence inputs.
- Ordinary claim writes cannot forge comparison/supplement state. Derived comparison data is excluded from scanning itself for adjuster contacts.
- Inventory, replacement approvals, buckets, requested totals, payments, received checks and PA fees are unchanged by reconciliation. Historical valued items may need separate new research; do not overwrite the old submitted baseline.
- Archive/reopen is operational queue management, not claim closure, carrier acceptance or payment verification.

## Activation and import bounds
Spreadsheet import and manual original transcription work without AI secrets. PDF AI extraction is implemented using existing OPENAI_API_KEY / OPENAI_MODEL (default gpt-4.1), but production currently has no key. José must activate it. One PDF <=12 MB, up to 100 extracted leads, 10 extraction attempts per hour per claim; results are unreviewed and do not imply full-report coverage. No tools or sending are available to the extraction model; documents/filenames are untrusted data.

XLSX reader uses bounded streaming ZIP decompression and validated XML, with no macro/formula execution, external fetching or DTD/entity handling. Limits: 8 MB compressed workbook; 24 MB declared expanded content; 16 MB selected XML; 4 MB per XML; 150 archive entries; 20 worksheets; 3000 physical source rows; 101 columns. Table import selects up to 2500 rows. CSV/text table <=2 MB. XLS/XLSM/encrypted files need conversion; formulas need static values or manual source-based transcription. Money is nonnegative USD with at most two decimals; blank is unknown. Carrier credits/adjustment/payment summaries must be reviewed separately, not treated as physical contents.

Claim state bounds: 12 comparisons, 2500 carrier rows per comparison, groups of at most 50 ADPA + 50 carrier rows, 100 recent revision entries, 30 supplement drafts and 200 recent audits, within the existing 1 MB claim JSON limit. Split unusually large cases/revisions into smaller supported work; no unlimited/certified legal archive is promised.
