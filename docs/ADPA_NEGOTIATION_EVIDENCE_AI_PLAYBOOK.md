# ADPA Negotiation, Evidence & AI Decision Playbook

## Core principle
The system should make a claim easier to prove, audit, and explain. It should not manufacture leverage. Leverage comes from policy language, documented facts, credible valuation, deadlines, undisputed amounts, and a clean chronology.

## Issue-file method
Every disagreement is classified as one or more of:
Coverage; Causation; Scope; Quantity; Quality/LKQ; Pricing; Depreciation; Code/Ordinance; Contents; ALE; Mitigation; Payment; Proof of Loss; Mortgage/Loss Draft.

Each issue file must contain:
- carrier position and exact source;
- ADPA position;
- carrier and ADPA dollar amounts / variance;
- policy/form/endorsement citation;
- evidence references;
- state-rule reference when relevant;
- missing proof;
- requested action;
- escalation level;
- licensed-review status;
- history of changes.

## Evidence-to-argument rule
No material outbound assertion should be presented as established fact unless the claim record contains a source or the reviewer explicitly marks it as professional judgment. AI must distinguish:
- VERIFIED FACT
- SOURCE-SUPPORTED INFERENCE
- PROFESSIONAL JUDGMENT — PA REVIEW REQUIRED
- MISSING EVIDENCE
- LEGAL INTERPRETATION — COUNSEL REVIEW
- UNKNOWN / ABSTAIN

## Dwelling negotiation
Compare carrier vs ADPA at room/trade/line level. Reconcile quantity, unit, material grade, labor, access, consequential damage, tax/O&P where applicable, code, permit, demolition, matching/LKQ, and source. Ask the carrier for the factual/estimating basis of each material adjustment rather than arguing only from totals.

## Contents negotiation
For each material item: ownership/source, location, description, make/model, age, condition, quantity, RCV source, LKQ match, depreciation basis, ACV, replacement proof, recoverable depreciation. Avoid false precision when age/model is estimated; label estimates.

## ALE
Separate normal baseline expense from increased expense. Tie each claimed expense to displacement/habitability and the relevant period of restoration. Track lease, utilities, food differential, storage, pets, transportation/mileage, receipts, and carrier approvals/limitations.

## Code / Ordinance
Never assert code merely because an estimator includes a line. Link the claim to the policy coverage, adopted provision, authority having jurisdiction, repair trigger, required scope, and cost evidence.

## Causation / expert gate
When causation turns on engineering, fire origin, environmental science, plumbing failure, structural movement, mold, or another expert domain, AI may organize evidence but should flag expert review rather than invent an expert conclusion.

## Negotiation ladder
1. Evidence / clarification.
2. Written reconsideration with exact issue and source.
3. Supervisor review with issue/dollar summary.
4. Formal dispute or supplement.
5. Licensed PA escalation review.
6. DOI complaint consideration.
7. Appraisal consideration.
8. Counsel review.

The appropriate path depends on policy, state law, issue type, and facts. Appraisal generally addresses amount-of-loss issues and must not be presented by AI as a universal coverage remedy.

## Carrier conduct chronology
Record facts, not accusations. Preferred language:
- "Our records reflect..."
- "Please identify the policy/estimate/evidence basis..."
- "The recorded response date is..."
- "Our file does not reflect receipt..."
- "The undisputed portion appears to be..."
Avoid unsupported labels such as bad faith, fraud, intentional delay, or statutory violation.

## AI release rules
AI can:
- extract/organize;
- compare estimates and payments;
- identify missing evidence;
- calculate provisional clocks;
- draft issue summaries and correspondence;
- recommend next workflow step.

AI cannot autonomously:
- bind coverage;
- settle a claim;
- accept a release;
- waive insured rights;
- give legal advice;
- sign as the PA/client;
- send a material negotiation position without required approval;
- fabricate a policy/statutory citation;
- turn an unverified deadline into a legal conclusion.

## Mandatory abstention
Use: "Insufficient verified policy language/evidence/authority for a reliable position. Licensed PA review required."  
Counsel escalation when the requested output depends on legal advice, litigation strategy, privilege, unsettled law, interpretation outside ordinary claim adjustment, or represented-party restrictions.

## Quality-control benchmark
Before expanding AI autonomy, test representative CA/NV claims for:
- jurisdiction accuracy;
- correct policy source;
- evidence-source fidelity;
- dollar reconciliation;
- deadline trigger and calculation;
- hallucinated citations;
- missed exceptions;
- human-review routing;
- package version/hash integrity;
- audit completeness.
