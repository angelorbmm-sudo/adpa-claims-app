# Contents inventory and care: public research basis

Checked October 8, 2026. This file contains public guidance and implementation rationale only. Private claim correspondence and detailed practitioner examples remain in the separately saved user research report.

## Public primary sources

- https://iicrc.org/s300/ — public scope of the published 2025 second edition covers upholstery construction and pre-/post-cleaning inspection. Capture material, compatible-method basis and before/after contractor review. Full paid standard was not accessed.
- https://iicrc.org/s700/ — public scope covers fire residues, odors, restoration work plans and contents. Unusual events and sensitive occupants need appropriate qualified review; this scope is not a universal safety certification.
- https://iicrc.org/s320/ — in development, with public review September 11–October 26, 2026. Do not label the module S320-compliant.
- https://www.epa.gov/mold/what-are-basic-mold-cleanup-steps — moisture control, material limitations and specialist consultation for uncertain or valuable items matter. Do not infer automatic disposal of all porous items or mandatory testing for every loss.
- https://www.insurance.ca.gov/01-consumers/105-type/95-guides/03-res/home-inv.cfm — room-based records, photographs, descriptions, serial/model information and alternative evidence where receipts are unavailable.
- https://contentsreco.com/services/ — company-described evaluation, inventory, pack-out, storage and return workflow. Promotional claims do not establish independently verified qualifications or guaranteed restoration outcomes.

## Implementation rationale

Preserve physical-item quantities, sets/boxes and item-specific evidence. Separate prior condition from loss damage. Track alternate reports as references to the same inventory, avoiding automatic additive quantities. Keep on-site manipulation, off-site custody, storage periods and return acceptance explicit. Require cleaning method/compatibility basis and before/after QA, with unresolved concerns flagged for qualified review. Preserve unknowns and conflicting source identifiers.

Keep preliminary/actual/supplemental service invoices separate from property valuations and payment records. Distinguish owner authorization, written carrier position, issued payment and received payment. Do not infer coverage, net demand, total loss, treatment prescriptions, safety/compliance certification or legal payment duties.

The dedicated authenticated module saves claim-specific records with version guards and human evidence review. Changes stale prior reviews. Preparation findings, internal package snapshots and approval blockers include outstanding contents-care issues.
