# Peter Rooney — Measurement Validation

For simple gable, hip, multi-valley, dormer-heavy and irregular/obscured roofs, independently review:
- source geometry, units and per-facet pitch
- sloped roof area and squares
- ridge, hip, valley, eave and rake lengths
- penetrations, flashing, underlayment and accessory assemblies
- waste rationale and product package coverage
- installed versus ordered quantities
- missing evidence, field verification and manual overrides
- accuracy tolerances defined before testing
- reviewer sign-off before estimate export

Do not upload private client measurements or proprietary source reports to public GitHub.
