# Divide my belongings — LifeWorth

Open LifeWorth → select property → Divide my belongings. Create a named plan with two distinct participant labels. Select contents or equipment records, then allocate whole units to either participant; remaining units stay undecided. Each inventory record appears once within a plan; the same item may appear in other alternative plans. Building materials are excluded.

Division values are manually entered per unit with a date and source and never modify inventory replacement values, quantities, purchase/refund records or coverage. Blank division values remain unpriced. Totals are proposed values across current selected lines, with unpriced items, unassigned units, disagreements and stale rows displayed separately. No fairness or equalization payment is inferred.

Each participant has a separate owner-recorded response (Pending, Agrees, Disagrees), original-source reference and response date. A disagreement makes the item Disputed. Agreed (owner-recorded) requires both responses and fully assigned quantities. This is a private planning workflow; independent participant authentication, collaboration invitations and e-signatures are not implemented or implied. It does not determine legal ownership or transfer title.

Material proposal edits archive prior responses and clear current responses. Participant label edits do the same. Relevant inventory edits archive responses and mark rows Needs refresh; outdated values are excluded from totals until the proposal is refreshed. Response history is preserved up to an enforced capacity of 100 entries per line, then edits are rejected rather than dropping history. Linked items/plans cannot be deleted or moved across properties without removing dependent proposals. Removing a proposal leaves its inventory item intact, but removes that proposal's response history after an explicit in-app warning.

CSV export includes only the selected plan, allocations, captured quantities, proposed unit values and source/date, status, each response/source/date and notes, with formula-neutralization. JSON export includes full records and response history. Existing owner authentication, same-origin checks, optimistic concurrency and atomic JSON persistence are reused. No existing records were seeded or changed by deployment.
